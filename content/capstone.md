Teams of 3–4 ship one **deployed translation tool for a real user** outside the classroom, and defend its translation choices on Demo Day.

## Team roles

- **Lead Translator** — quality, test set, glossary.
- **Product Builder** — prompts, app, deployment.
- **User Researcher** — finds the real user, runs tests, collects feedback.
- **Documenter** — README, About page, reflection report, Demo Day slides.

## Deliverables

1. One-page product brief (Week 13): user, problem, input, output, engine choice and why, risks.
2. A test set of at least 30 sentences with expected translations.
3. The deployed app with a public link and a GitHub repository.
4. An evaluation: MQM error counts before and after prompt fixes, plus one engine comparison.
5. A 1,000-word reflection linking three design decisions to translation theory.
6. A 5-minute Demo Day presentation and a 3-minute live demo.

## Ideas rooted in Kerala and Calicut

| Idea | Real user | Engines to try | DH angle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Panchayat Notice Simplifier | Elderly residents, ward members | Gemini + Sarvam | Plain language as public service |
| Campus Bilingual Signage Kit | College administration | Gemini vision | Linguistic landscape |
| Malabar Heritage Explainer | Local museums, heritage walks | Gemini + Bhashini TTS | Digital public history |
| Public-domain Malayalam poetry reader (e.g. Kumaran Asan) | Students, literature teachers | Gemini + human-edited notes | Digital scholarly edition |
| Code-mixed (Manglish) Helpdesk Translator | Small shops, local services | Sarvam code-mixed mode | Sociolinguistics |
| Health Leaflet Translator with glossary | A local PHC or NSS unit | Sarvam + Glossary Builder | Health communication |
| Oral History Transcriber and Translator | History department, elders' interviews | Bhashini ASR + MT | Oral archives, consent |
| Subtitle studio for student films | College film club | Subtitle Studio + human edit | AV translation corpus |
| Gulf migrant letter and form helper | Migrant families | Gemini + Sarvam | Migration and language |
| Department translation memory | Future batches | Sheets + Hugging Face | Reusable corpus |

**Rule for sensitive users:** health, legal and government tools show a clear "check with an official or professional" notice, and a human expert reviews the final test set.
