## Indian language AI stack

| Platform | What it gives you | Notes | Week |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Bhashini](https://bhashini.gov.in) | Free government APIs: translation, speech-to-text, text-to-speech, transliteration | National Language Translation Mission (2022); 22 scheduled languages; ULCA standard for data and models | 8 |
| [Sarvam AI](https://docs.sarvam.ai) | Translation API with formal / colloquial / code-mixed modes, gender control | Mayura: 11 languages incl. Malayalam, 1,000 chars per request. Sarvam-Translate: 22 languages, 2,000 chars. Free trial | 9 |
| [AIKosh](https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/) | IndiaAI's repository of datasets, models, use cases | Launched March 2025; browse and download after sign-up | 10 |
| [AI4Bharat IndicTrans2](https://github.com/AI4Bharat/IndicTrans2) | Open translation models for 22 scheduled languages | Runs on Hugging Face; distilled models suit free hardware | 10 |
| [IndicMT Eval](https://github.com/AI4Bharat/IndicMT-Eval) | Human error annotations (MQM) incl. Malayalam | Basis for Week 11 quality lesson | 11 |

## Digital Humanities bridge

- Glossaries and corpora → corpus building and text analysis (try [Voyant Tools](https://voyant-tools.org)).
- Snap & Translate, poetry reader → digitisation and scholarly editions (TEI).
- Engine comparison and slop → critical AI studies: whose data, whose standards.
- Oral history capstone → archives, consent, community ethics.

## Ethics rules

- Every app says its output is machine-generated and names the engine.
- No real personal data in prompts; test with invented or public texts.
- Use public-domain or department texts; no whole copyrighted works.
- Keys never on slides, screenshots, chat or GitHub. A leaked key is replaced the same day.
- Students note where and how AI helped in each submission.
- Health, legal and government texts are always reviewed by a qualified human.
